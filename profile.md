## Seniority calibration

Title does not matter to her (owner quiz, 2026-10-26): a project engineer
or project manager who owns delivery of a new plant is as good a fit as a
director. She is a manager today with 10+ years of experience, a PMP, and
has managed 50+ people across concurrent programs.

- Judge seniority from the DESCRIBED SCOPE, not the title alone. Owning a
  project, a program, a region or a P&L, managing teams or leading vendor
  and OEM relationships reads senior even under a flat title
  ("Project Engineer" at a hyperscaler frequently is).
- Senior Engineer, Lead Engineer and Principal Engineer titles are fine
  when the scope is ownership of a project or a technology area. Individual
  contributor is not a disqualifier; the work's subject matter is.
- VP and C-level roles score mid-70s at most unless the described scope
  reads attainable.
- Genuinely junior, early-career or trade-level roles (Associate Engineer,
  entry-level PM, technician, apprentice): seniority_match=false.
